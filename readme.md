# OpenStrm

A free Android TV streaming app - IPTV Player with Stremio support.

## ❤️ Why I Created OpenStrm

I'm a big fan of **TiviMate**. It's one of my favourite IPTV players, but there was one thing that i never able to play two stream together in MultiView.

So I thought everone building IPTV... why not build my own player?

That's how **OpenStrm** started.

While developing I though it would be good idea to support **Stremio**, so I decided to bring that subset of stremio [note it has no P2P support]. Now, alongside IPTV streaming, there's an option to enjoy Stremio content (with the required plugin).

At start I was not going to release it but after a while i though its good enough for me then someone might enjoy it or other app might add those feature after watching my app :D


And yes, **it's 100% free!** 😁

## ✨ Features

### 📺 MultiView

Watch multiple streams simultaneously on a single screen.

Some TVs, including my Sony Bravia, cannot decode two video streams simultaneously using their hardware decoder.

There's a workaround!

1. Open MultiView.
2. Go to **Advanced Settings**.
3. Change the video decoder from Hardware to **Software**.
4. Enjoy multiple streams together!

This can help TVs that have limited hardware video-decoding capabilities.

### 🪟 Picture-in-Picture (PiP)

Keep watching your stream in a floating window while using other apps.

- Browse other apps without stopping your stream.
- Keep your video playing while doing something else.
- Enjoy a more flexible multitasking experience.

**Having trouble with PiP on your TV?**

If your TV doesn't support multiple hardware video decoders, switch the PiP video decoder to **Software** in PiP settings.

### 🎬 Stremio Support


OpenStrm includes Stremio support, allowing you to enjoy the Stremio experience alongside your IPTV streaming.

**Note:** You'll need the required OpenStrm plugin to play titles through Stremio.

### 🆓 100% Free

No subscriptions. No paid unlocks. No premium tiers.

OpenStrm is free to use. I built it because I wanted a better streaming experience, not another subscription.
